# The quick-fire sweep: the odd ones in finding work

Run this in Part 6 (Q22 to Q25), after the normal way is down and the step-by-step "is it always
like this?" (Q21) is done. Its job is to catch the cases an assistant gets wrong because nobody
thought to mention them.

## How to run it

- **Four rounds, one message each.** Each round counts as **one** question toward the 35.
- **Drop any line already covered.** Skip round 2 if they don't chase tenders or builders, and
  round 3 if no work comes to them through Google, reviews or referrals. Say so in one line.
- Show the round as a short numbered list, then ask: **"Which of these happen to you? Just say the
  numbers, and a word on what you do."**
- Lines they don't pick are recorded as `doesn't happen`. Lines they pick get **one line** each in
  the map file. Only if a pick opens a new rule, ask **one** short ↳ follow-up (it counts).
- Start the round with its name, e.g. `Odd ones, round 2 of 4 · tenders and builders`.

Examples in italics are from the made-up business, Tallowood Painting.

## Round 1 of 4 · where work turns up (Q22)

1. **Too much at once:** tender alerts or enquiries pile up faster than you read them? _(40
   EstimateOne emails a week; I read maybe ten.)_
2. **Busy weeks:** you stop looking for work when you're flat out, then go quiet? _(Every winter.)_
3. **Wrong fit:** leads that aren't your kind of work (wrong size, wrong area, wrong trade)? _(Lots
   of new-build apartments; we don't do those.)_
4. **The same job twice:** the same project from two builders or two portals? _(Often; three
   builders tender the same job.)_
5. **Out of season:** some work only comes at certain times of year? _(Exteriors in spring.)_
6. **Word of mouth you never hear about:** a referral that rang someone else? _(Found out at a
   barbecue.)_

## Round 2 of 4 · tenders and builders (Q23)

1. **Price checks:** a builder sends it to everyone just to check a price? _(If it's 12 painters,
   it's a price check.)_
2. **No drawings yet:** the job's real but nothing to price? _(Half the tenders.)_
3. **Who's the estimator:** it changes, or you can't find them? _(Builder B changes estimators every
   year.)_
4. **Getting on the list:** builders who need forms, insurances or a site induction first? _(Builder A
   wants our insurances every year.)_
5. **Slow payers:** builders you'd rather not chase? _(Two we won't work for.)_
6. **Tender closes:** you miss the closing date? _(Twice this year.)_
7. **Follow-up that annoys:** chasing too often loses the builder? _(Once a fortnight is the most.)_

## Round 3 of 4 · work that comes to you (Q24)

1. **Bad review:** someone posts one, and who answers it? _(One last year; I replied the next week.)_
2. **No photos:** you finish a job and never take the after photo? _(Most jobs.)_
3. **Referral thanks:** do you thank or reward whoever referred? _(Never.)_
4. **Old customers:** past customers you could ring but don't have their details? _(They're in old
   invoices in Xero.)_
5. **Ads with no answer:** an ad brings enquiries you can't answer fast enough? _(Turned the ads off
   in winter.)_
6. **Tyre kickers:** enquiries that were never going to go ahead? _(Students in rentals.)_

## Round 4 of 4 · people, records and when things break (Q25)

1. **If you're off for two weeks:** who chases work, and what only you know? _(Nobody. Who to ring at
   each builder is in my head.)_
2. **Typing it twice:** every place a lead gets typed again (email → spreadsheet → phone)? _(Tender
   email into the sheet, then the sheet into my phone.)_
3. **Messy list:** the same builder twice, no area or type column, people who've left? _(Half the
   estimators have moved on.)_
4. **Where the truth lives:** if the list, the email and your memory disagree, which wins? _(My
   memory.)_
5. **Lost track:** a job you were chasing just disappeared? _(The Killara townhouses.)_
6. **Which source paid:** do you know where each job you won came from? _(Not really.)_

## Where the answers go

In the map file's **Exceptions and odd ones** table: one row per line they picked, with the step it
touches, what they do now, any rule or check it creates, and where it came from (`sweep 1`, `Q21`,
`test case`). Lines not picked are summed up in one sentence ("Didn't apply: …"). Anything that
creates a check also goes under **Checks for your assistant**, and each exception that changes a
step goes in that step's `odd_cases` on the map.
