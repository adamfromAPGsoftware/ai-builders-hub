# The quick-fire sweep: the odd ones in quoting

Run this in Part 6 (Q28 to Q31), after the normal way is down and the step-by-step "is it always
like this?" (Q27) is done. Its job is to catch the cases a tool gets wrong because nobody thought to
mention them.

## How to run it

- **Four rounds, one message each.** Each round counts as **one** question toward the 35, however
  many lines it has.
- **Drop any line already covered** by their quotes or earlier answers. If a whole round is
  covered, skip it and say so in one line.
- Show the round as a short numbered list in plain words, then ask: **"Which of these happen to
  you? Just say the numbers, and a word on what you do."** Voice answers like "two, we just wing it;
  five, happens all the time" are perfect.
- Lines they don't pick are recorded as `doesn't happen`. Lines they pick get **one line** each in
  the file. Only if a pick opens a new pricing rule, ask **one** short ↳ follow-up (it counts).
- Don't explain the lines. If they ask "what do you mean?", give the Tallowood example.
- "Don't know" goes on the parking list with who could answer it.
- Start the round with its name, e.g. `Odd ones, round 2 of 4 · bad info, changes and timing`.

Examples in italics are from the made-up business, Tallowood Painting.

## Round 1 of 4 · jobs that aren't the usual (Q28)

1. **Size:** tiny jobs or big jobs priced or handled differently? _(Minimum $650 ex GST; over
   $10,000 ex GST I set the price myself.)_
2. **Who it's for:** homeowner vs builder vs strata or real estate vs commercial: different
   process, not just a different price? _(Strata: the manager sends photos, no visit, and wants
   three options.)_
3. **Insurance work:** the insurer's scope, an assessor, or their rates instead of yours? _(Don't
   do it.)_
4. **Rush jobs:** quote needed today, or the job needs doing this week. Charge more? Skip steps?
   _(I'll quote off photos and say so; no rush fee.)_
5. **Repeat customers or the same house again:** do you start from the last quote? _(Same
   builder's next house, I copy the last one and change the rooms.)_
6. **Same job to several builders:** one copy each, same price, their name on it? _(Yes, two or
   three copies.)_
7. **Same customer through different people:** an agent, strata manager or builder asks for one
   owner. Who's the customer, and who gets the invoice? _(Agent asks, owner pays.)_
8. **Sites with rules:** schools, strata, after-hours only, sign-in, inductions, escorts, no
   photos. _(Occupied units: weekdays 8 to 4 only.)_
9. **Extra work after they say yes:** how is it priced and written down? _(Text the customer a
   price, add it to the invoice. Nothing written.)_
10. **Flat out:** when you're busiest, what gets dropped? _(Small jobs don't get quoted and
    follow-ups stop.)_
11. **Half an enquiry:** a call with no address, no sizes, "just ballpark it"? _(I give a range by
    phone and don't write it down.)_

## Round 2 of 4 · bad info, changes and timing (Q29)

1. **No plans, no photos, nothing:** do you still quote? _(Only after a visit.)_
2. **Wrong sizes:** plans or the customer's sizes don't match what's there? _(Plans say 2.4 m
   ceilings, house has 2.7 m: I measure and use mine.)_
3. **They count it differently:** the customer says "four rooms", you price square metres; a
   builder's schedule groups things your way doesn't. How do you regroup? _(I price by m² and show
   a price per room.)_
4. **Old prices:** your rate sheet or supplier list is out of date. Who notices? _(I notice when
   the invoice comes in.)_
5. **Can't get it:** a product or colour out of stock or discontinued after you've quoted?
   _(Swap to the nearest match and tell them.)_
6. **Can't see it:** what's under or behind (lead paint, rot, flaking, hazardous material). What
   do you write? _(Assumed no lead paint unless tested.)_
7. **Revised quote:** the customer changes the scope. New quote number, or the same one with a
   version? How fast must it go back? _(Same number with "rev B", usually a day or two.)_
8. **Accepted late:** they say yes after the quote's expired, or months later. Same price?
   _(Over 30 days I re-check the paint price.)_
9. **Gone quiet:** how many chases, how, when do you give up? _(I mean to ring after a week.)_
10. **Deadlines:** a builder's tender closing date; quoting at night or on weekends? _(Builders
    want it in 5 working days; I write quotes Sunday night.)_
11. **Next stage of the same job:** quoted from the old info without anyone re-checking? _(Stage 2
    of a reno priced off the stage 1 plans.)_

## Round 3 of 4 · money and decisions (Q30)

1. **Written down or in your head:** the price, the margin, which supplier, whether to quote at
   all? _(Rates written; the "is it worth it" call is in my head.)_
2. **Who can say yes to a price:** any dollar amount where it needs checking or your sign-off? Who
   covers when you're away? _(Only me. When I'm away, quotes wait.)_
3. **Inc or ex GST mix-ups:** customers comparing your ex GST price with someone's inc GST price?
   _(Happens; homeowners now see the inc GST total in bold.)_
4. **Deposits, progress payments, retention:** different on builder jobs? _(Builders: monthly
   progress claims, 5% retention.)_
5. **Supplier terms:** does your trade account, rebate or payment terms change how you price?
   _(No.)_
6. **Rounding:** do you round the total to a nice number? Up or down? _(Round to the nearest $10
   ex GST.)_
7. **One-off deals:** a special price for one customer, without changing your list? _(A
   "discount" line with the reason.)_
8. **Someone's cheaper:** do you ever match or drop the price? Who decides? _(Never under my
   minimum; I decide.)_

## Round 4 of 4 · people, records and when things break (Q31)

1. **If you're off for two weeks:** who quotes? What only you know? _(No one. Only I know the
   rates for odd jobs.)_
2. **Typing it twice:** every place a number gets typed again (pad → Excel → email → Xero).
   _(Room sizes typed twice; total typed again into Xero for the deposit.)_
3. **Messy records:** the same customer saved twice, the same item named differently on different
   quotes, two versions of the price list. Which one is right? _(The Excel rate sheet on my
   laptop is the right one.)_
4. **When it breaks:** laptop dies, the template breaks, a quote's sent to the wrong person. What
   then? _(Template's on my laptop only.)_
5. **Changed after yes:** a quote edited after the customer accepted it? _(Sometimes, for extras;
   no record of the original.)_
6. **Did the price hold up:** does anyone compare what the job really cost with the quote, and
   update the rates? _(Only when one goes badly.)_
7. **Done means:** when a quote's won or lost, or the job starts, who's told and how? _(Win: I
   text the leading hand. Lost: no one.)_

## Where the answers go

In the file's **Exceptions and edge cases** table (see `intake-file.md`): one row per line they
picked, with what they do, any rule it creates (R number), and where it came from (`sweep`, `Q27`,
`test case`). Lines not picked are summed up in one sentence ("Didn't apply: …"). Anything that
creates a check for the tool also goes under **What they want the tool to do** as a check, and each
exception that changes a step gets a red marker on the drawing.
